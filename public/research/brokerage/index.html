Powered by Alpaca

A comprehensive technical design for integrating US Equities trading into the platform, leveraging the existing wallet system and KYC infrastructure.

01 — The Goal

Bridging Wallets and Wall Street

The Brokerage Module enables users to open broker accounts, fund them from their local wallet, and trade US equities directly within the app.

Product Path: We are launching with Path A — an Alpaca-backed brokerage for US equities, allowing users to fund investments from their NGN wallet while trading in USD.
The Partnership

Alpaca Broker API

Alpaca provides the full brokerage lifecycle: account opening, clearing, custody, and trade execution. Our module acts as the orchestration layer between Alpaca and our core systems.

Asset Scope

US Equities (LCT)

Leveraging Local Currency Trading (LCT) to allow Nigerian users to trade US stocks using their local currency (NGN) wallet balance as the ultimate source of funds.

02 — Core Features

The Investment Experience

Built to be seamless, the module covers everything from regulatory suitability to fractional trading.

Feature B-01

Broker Onboarding

Risk-profile suitability questionnaire, tax residency capture, and disclosure acceptance mapped to Alpaca's requirements.

Feature B-02

Wallet Funding

Direct journal movements from the platform wallet to brokerage cash. Supports deposits and withdrawals back to local bank accounts.

Feature B-03

Fractional Trading

Buy stocks by dollar amount instead of share count. Lowers the barrier to entry for users with smaller wallet balances.

Feature B-04

Portfolio Insights

Real-time unrealized PnL, market value tracking, and position snapshots synced with Alpaca's ledger.

Feature B-05

Regulatory Docs

Automated retrieval of monthly statements, trade confirmations, and tax documents (1099/8-2-1) via API.

Feature B-06

Real-time Alerts

Push/SMS notifications for order fills, account approvals, and funding status updates driven by SSE events.

03 — User Journey

Onboarding & Compliance

We reuse existing KYC data while capturing additional broker-specific identifiers and suitability profiles.

1

Capture Suitability Data

User provides employment status, income range, net worth, and investment experience. This is required for FINRA/SEC compliance.

2

Tax & Identity Mapping

Capture Tax ID (TIN) and confirm country of tax residence. Map existing government ID records to Alpaca's onboarding schema.

3

Disclosure Acceptance

User reviews and accepts Alpaca's account agreement, margin disclosure (even for cash accounts), and risk disclosures.

4

Alpaca Account Creation

Our backend calls `POST /v1/accounts` on Alpaca. If `ACTION_REQUIRED`, we trigger a document upload flow in-app.

04 — Funding Flow

Wallet Integration

Security-first cash management. We treat the app wallet as the source and Alpaca's cash ledger as the destination.

Ledger Isolation: We never treat wallet balance and brokerage cash as the same ledger. Every movement is a recorded journal event with multi-stage verification.
sequenceDiagram actor U as User participant W as App Wallet participant B as Brokerage Module participant A as Alpaca Broker API U->>B: Request Deposit (e.g. 50,000 NGN) B->>B: Validate PIN & KYC Status B->>W: Place Reserve/Hold (Pending) B->>A: POST /v1/accounts/{id}/transfers (LCT) A-->>B: Transfer Confirmed B->>W: Finalize Debit (Settled) B->>B: Mark Movement as COMPLETED B-->>U: 🎉 Funds available for trading
05 — Trading Engine

Order Lifecycle & Fractional Trading

The core of the brokerage experience. We optimize for high-availability quotes and low-latency order submission.

Execution Model

Market & Limit Orders

Support for instant market execution and price-sensitive limit orders. Users can specify "Time in Force" (Day vs GTC) to manage their trading strategy effectively.

Fractional Shares

Notional-based Investing

Allowing users to invest as little as $1 in expensive stocks like Amazon or Google. Our engine calculates the exact fractional share count based on the current market price.

08 — Security

Safety & Risk Mitigation

Protecting both user assets and the platform's regulatory standing.

Two-Factor Auth

Transaction PIN + Biometrics

Requiring the existing user transaction PIN for every order placement and funding withdrawal. Layered with device-level biometrics for secondary verification.

Risk Controls

Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

Continuous monitoring of account activity for suspicious patterns, integrated with Alpaca's own compliance engine and local regulatory checklists.
